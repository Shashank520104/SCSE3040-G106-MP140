6. DETAILED INVESTIGATION OF OBSERVED DATA QUALITY ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 1: UNIT MISMATCH IN km_driven (Central Zone)
  - Status: CONFIRMED PRESENT
  - Affected Group: city_zone == 'Central'
  - Affected Records: 273 rows (100% of Central zone rows)
  - Central Zone km_driven: Min = 1.11, Median = 47.32, Mean = 47.09, Max = 89.99
  - North Zone km_driven:   Min = 1,427, Median = 47,575, Mean = 46,878, Max = 89,877
  - South Zone km_driven:   Min = 1,159, Median = 44,234, Mean = 43,530, Max = 89,607
  - Diagnostic: Central zone recorded distances in thousand km (e.g. 47.32 instead of 47,320 km).
  - Unit Factor: 0.001 (wrong_value = right_value * 0.001). Correct by multiplying Central values by 1000.

ISSUE 2: SENTINEL VALUES IN owners (-1)
  - Status: CONFIRMED PRESENT
  - Affected Column: 'owners'
  - Sentinel Value: -1
  - Affected Records: 63 rows (6.3% of training data)
  - Value Breakdown: -1: 63, 1: 173, 2: 324, 3: 292, 4: 148
  - Diagnostic: -1 is an impossible owner count, used as a placeholder for unknown/unrecorded owners.

ISSUE 3: STUCK SENSOR IN engine_cc (119 cc)
  - Status: CONFIRMED PRESENT
  - Affected Column: 'engine_cc'
  - Stuck Value: 119 cc
  - Consecutive Stuck Run: 71 consecutive rows
  - Date Range: 2026-03-05 to 2026-03-18 (14 continuous days)
  - Affected Record IDs: R00383 through R00451
  - Total Occurrences of 119 cc: 72 (71 consecutive + 1 isolated on 2026-06-21)
  - Diagnostic: Data logger / sensor malfunctioned between 05-Mar-2026 and 18-Mar-2026, logging 119 for every entry.
